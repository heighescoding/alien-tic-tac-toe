How to play

Choose any empty square. A short alien battle decides who claims it. Win the battle to place X. Lose it and the aliens place O. There is no extra alien move.

Each battle starts with 2 health for you and 2 for the alien. Choose one response per exchange. Correct counters damage the alien; all other choices damage you.

Three X marks in a row wins the board. Three O marks means the aliens win. A full board without a line is a draw. The Restart game button clears the entire board and any ongoing battle.
